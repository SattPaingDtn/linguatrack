## Lesson 2 — Grammar foundations

### Grammar explanation

**Present simple for habits:** `I start work at ten.` / `She starts work at nine.` Add `-s` or `-es` with he/she/it in affirmative clauses: `He watches TV.`

**Negative:** `I do not (don't) work on Sunday.` / `She does not (doesn't) work on Sunday.` After `doesn't`, use the **base form**: `doesn't work`, not `doesn't works`.

**Question:** `Do you work from home?` / `Does she work from home?` Answers: `Yes, I do.` / `No, she doesn't.`

**Time:** `at 8:30`, `on Monday`, `in the morning`, `every day`. `Usually`, `often`, `sometimes`, and `never` commonly go before a main verb: `I usually study at night.` With `be`: `She is usually early.`

**မြန်မာလို:** နေ့တိုင်းလုပ်နေကျ၊ အလေ့အကျင့်ကို ပြောချင်ရင် Present Simple သုံးတယ်။ `I work` ဆိုပေမဲ့ `She works` လို့ `-s` ထည့်ရတယ်။ မလုပ်ဘူးဆိုရင် `don't/doesn't`၊ မေးချင်ရင် `Do/Does` သုံးတယ်။ `doesn't` နောက်မှာ Verb မူရင်းပုံစံပဲ သုံးပါ (`doesn't work`)။ အချိန်ပြတဲ့အခါ နာရီနဲ့ `at`၊ နေ့နဲ့ `on`၊ မနက်/ညနေနဲ့ `in` သုံးတယ်။

### Common mistakes

- Correct: `She works at nine.` / Incorrect: `She work at nine.`
- Correct: `He doesn't work on Sunday.` / Incorrect: `He doesn't works on Sunday.`
- Correct: `Does she study English?` / Incorrect: `Does she studies English?`
- Correct: `I start at 10:00.` / Incorrect: `I start on 10:00.`

**Grammar practice:** Explain the difference between the correct and incorrect versions above in Burmese. Then make four new sentences.

## Lesson 3 — Listening practice (scripts awaiting audio)

**Learner instruction:** Listen twice before opening the transcript. A script alone is not a listening assessment.

### Dialogue 1: Morning schedule

**Transcript for TTS / recording — keep hidden until learner attempts questions:**

> A: What time do you start work?
> B: I usually start at ten in the morning.
> A: Do you work from home?
> B: Yes, I do. But I go to the office on Wednesday.
> A: What time do you finish?
> B: I finish at seven in the evening.

**Comprehension questions:**

1. When does B usually start work?  A) At nine  B) At ten  C) At eleven
2. When does B go to the office?  A) Monday  B) Wednesday  C) Friday
3. Does B usually work from home?  A) Yes  B) No  C) Not stated

### Dialogue 2: Study routine

**Transcript for TTS / recording — keep hidden until learner attempts questions:**

> A: Does your sister study English?
> B: Yes, she does. She studies after dinner.
> A: Does she study every night?
> B: No, she doesn’t. She studies on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

**Comprehension questions:**

1. When does she study?  A) Before breakfast  B) After dinner  C) At lunchtime
2. Does she study every night?  A) Yes  B) No  C) Not stated

**မြန်မာလို:** Audio ရပြီးမှ မျက်နှာပြင်ကို မကြည့်ဘဲ နားထောင်ပါ။ နှစ်ကြိမ် နားထောင်ပြီးမှ မေးခွန်းတွေ ဖြေပါ။ အသံဖိုင်မရှိသေးရင် ဒီပိုင်းကို Script Reading Practice အနေနဲ့ပဲ သတ်မှတ်ပါ။

## Lesson 4 — Speaking and role-play

**Speak first; correct after finishing.**

1. Describe your usual weekday using five times and at least two frequency adverbs.
2. Role-play: ask a colleague “What time do you start?”, “Do you work from home?”, and “When do you finish?” Switch roles.
3. Record a one-minute routine. Listen again and check he/she -s, at/on/in, and do/does.

**Self-review:** Can I speak without stopping for 30 seconds? Are my verbs and pronouns correct? Could another person understand me?
**မြန်မာလို:** အမှားကြောက်ပြီး မရပ်ပါနဲ့။ ပြောပြီးမှ Grammar ကို ပြန်စစ်ပါ။

## Lesson 5 — Reading comprehension

### Passage 1: A developer’s weekday

I am Lin. I work as a front-end developer in Japan. I start work at 10:00 a.m. I usually work from home. At noon, I eat lunch. I have a short team meeting at 2:00 p.m. I finish at 7:00 p.m. I sometimes study English after dinner.

**Questions:**

1. What time does Lin start?  A) 9:00  B) 10:00  C) 11:00
2. Where does Lin usually work?  A) From home  B) At a café  C) In a hospital
3. When is the team meeting?  A) Noon  B) 2:00 p.m.  C) 7:00 p.m.

### Passage 2: Saturday routine

On Saturdays, Mika wakes up at eight. She eats breakfast and goes to a language class at ten. She does not work on Saturday. She usually reads in the afternoon and calls her family in the evening.

**Questions:**

1. Does Mika work on Saturday?  A) Yes  B) No  C) Not stated
2. What does she usually do in the afternoon?  A) Read  B) Call her family  C) Work

**Reading strategy:** Underline the words that directly support your answer before selecting an option.

## Lesson 6 — Writing and editing

1. Write 7–9 sentences about your day. Include three clock times, one negative statement and one question.
2. Rewrite: “She start work at ten.” “He doesn’t goes to the office.” “Does she works from home?”

### Model paragraph (one possible answer)

I wake up at seven. I eat breakfast at eight. I start work at ten. I usually work from home. I have lunch at noon. I finish at seven. I do not work on Sunday. Do you work from home?

**မြန်မာလို:** Model answer က တစ်ခုတည်းသော အဖြေမှန် မဟုတ်ဘူး။ ကိုယ့်အကြောင်းနဲ့ ကိုက်ညီအောင် ရေးလို့ရတယ်။ Verb agreement, word order နဲ့ spelling ကို ကိုယ့်ဘာသာ ပြန်စစ်ပါ။

### Answer key — TEACHER / PRIVATE ONLY

#### Knowledge check
1. B — starts. She takes starts in an affirmative present-simple statement.
2. C — work. With I, use base form work.
3. A — doesn’t. He takes does not / doesn’t.
4. B — work. After doesn’t use base form.
5. A — Do. Do forms a present-simple question with you.
6. C — Does. Use Does with he.
7. B — at. At is used with clock time.
8. A — on. On is used with days.
