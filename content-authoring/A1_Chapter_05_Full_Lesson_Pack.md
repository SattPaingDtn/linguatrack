## Lesson 2 — Grammar foundations / သဒ္ဒါအခြေခံ

### Countable and uncountable

**English:** Countable: an egg, two eggs, three bananas. Uncountable in this context: water, rice, milk. Use a bottle of water or a bowl of rice to count containers/servings.

**မြန်မာလို:** egg, banana တို့ကို တစ်လုံး၊ နှစ်လုံး ရေတွက်နိုင်တယ်။ water, rice, milk တို့က ပုံမှန်အသုံးမှာ မရေတွက်တဲ့ noun ဖြစ်တယ်။ တိုင်းတာရင် a bottle of water လိုပြောပါ။

### Some / Any

**English:** Use some in many positive statements: I have some rice. Use any in many questions/negatives: Do you have any milk? I don’t have any milk. Polite offers/requests can use some: Can I have some water?

**မြန်မာလို:** ရိုးရိုးအပြောဝါကျမှာ some၊ မေးခွန်းနဲ့ အငြင်းမှာ any ကို မကြာခဏ သုံးတယ်။ ယဉ်ကျေးစွာ တောင်းတဲ့အခါ Can I have some water? လို့ သုံးနိုင်တယ်။

### How much / How many

**English:** How many eggs do you want? (countable plural). How much water do you want? (uncountable). How much is this sandwich? asks price.

**မြန်မာလို:** အရေအတွက်ရေတွက်ရလျှင် How many၊ မရေတွက်တဲ့အရာနဲ့ စျေးနှုန်းမေးလျှင် How much သုံးပါ။

### Polite ordering

**English:** I’d like a sandwich, please. / Could I have some water, please? / That’s all, thank you.

**မြန်မာလို:** စားသောက်ဆိုင်မှာ I’d like... / Could I have... ကိုသုံးရင် ပိုယဉ်ကျေးတယ်။

### Common mistakes / အမှားများ

| Incorrect ❌ | Correct ✅ |
|---|---|
| I want two rice. | I want two bowls of rice. |
| How many water? | How much water? |
| Do you have some milk? | Do you have any milk? (neutral question) |
| I would like an banana. | I would like a banana. |

**Apply:** Correct those sentences without looking. Then say three true sentences about your own life.

## Lesson 3 — Listening preparation / နားထောင်လေ့ကျင့်ရေး

**Audio required:** These are ORIGINAL scripts, not recordings. Once audio is generated, listen twice without the transcript; check answers; then reveal the transcript. Do not record a listening score from reading alone.

### Dialogue 1 — Ordering lunch

**Transcript (hide until after listening):**

- **Staff:** Hello. What would you like?
- **Customer:** I’d like chicken and rice, please.
- **Staff:** Would you like a drink?
- **Customer:** Could I have some water, please?
- **Staff:** Of course. Anything else?
- **Customer:** No, thank you. That’s all.

**Comprehension checks:**

1. What food does the customer order?
2. What drink does the customer request?

### Dialogue 2 — At the grocery store

**Transcript (hide until after listening):**

- **Customer:** How much are these bananas?
- **Clerk:** They are 300 yen for three.
- **Customer:** Okay. I’ll take three bananas.
- **Clerk:** Would you like a bag?
- **Customer:** No, thank you.

**Comprehension checks:**

1. How much are three bananas?
2. Does the customer need a bag?

**Shadowing:** Replay each line and repeat the speaker’s rhythm. / အသံထွက်ပြီးနောက် နောက်ကလိုက်ပြောပါ။

## Lesson 5 — Reading / စာဖတ်ခြင်း

### A simple shopping list

Ken goes to the supermarket after work. He needs two eggs, three bananas and a bottle of water. He has some rice at home, so he does not buy rice. He wants milk, but the shop does not have any today. The bananas cost 300 yen for three.

**မြန်မာလို လမ်းညွှန်ချက်:** ပထမတစ်ခေါက် အဘိဓာန်မကြည့်ဘဲ ဖတ်ပါ။ ဒုတိယအခေါက် အချက်အလက်တွေကို ရှာပြီး အောက်ပါမေးခွန်းတွေ ဖြေပါ။

**Questions:**

1. When does Ken go shopping?
2. How many eggs does he need?
3. Does he buy rice?
4. Does the store have milk?
5. How much do three bananas cost?

## Lesson 6 — Writing / စာရေးလေ့ကျင့်ခြင်း

**Task:** Write a short order (4–6 sentences) for a cafe or restaurant and a three-item shopping list. Use I’d like, some/any and how much or how many.

**မြန်မာလို:** ကိုယ်ပိုင်အခြေအနေကို အသုံးပြုပြီး ညွှန်ကြားချက်အတိုင်း ဝါကျများရေးပါ။ နမူနာကို မကူးပါနဲ့။
