## Lesson 2 — Grammar foundations

### Grammar explanation

**Have / has:** I, you, we, they **have**. He, she, it **has**. `I have a brother.` / `She has a laptop.`

**Possessive adjectives:** my, your, his, her, its, our, their come **before** a noun. `This is my bag.` / `That is her desk.` We do not say `This is me bag.`

**Singular/plural nouns:** `one book → two books`, `one child → two children`. After a singular countable noun, use an article or another determiner: `I have a sister.` Not `I have sister.`

**Be vs have:** `She is a teacher.` (identity); `She has a laptop.` (possession). Do not say `She is a laptop.` to mean ownership.

**မြန်မာလို:** ပိုင်ဆိုင်တာကို ပြောတဲ့အခါ `have/has` သုံးတယ်။ `I/you/we/they` နဲ့ `have`၊ `he/she/it` နဲ့ `has` တွဲတယ်။ `my/your/his/her/our/their` တွေက ပိုင်ရှင်ကိုညွှန်းတဲ့စကားလုံးဖြစ်ပြီး နာမ်ရှေ့မှာ ရှိတယ်။ `my phone`, `her book` လို့သုံးတယ်။ `is` က လူဘယ်သူလဲ/ဘယ်လိုလဲဆိုတာ ဖော်ပြတယ်၊ `has` က ပိုင်တာကိုဖော်ပြတယ်။

### Examples and typical mistakes

- Correct: `My sister has a phone.` / Incorrect: `My sister have a phone.`
- Correct: `This is his bag.` / Incorrect: `This is he bag.`
- Correct: `They are my friends.` / Incorrect: `They is my friends.`
- Correct: `I have two children.` / Incorrect: `I have two childs.`

**Grammar practice:** Explain the difference between the correct and incorrect versions above in Burmese. Then make four new sentences.

## Lesson 3 — Listening practice (scripts awaiting audio)

**Learner instruction:** Listen twice before opening the transcript. A script alone is not a listening assessment.

### Dialogue 1: Meet my family

**Transcript for TTS / recording — keep hidden until learner attempts questions:**

> A: Who is this in the photo?
> B: This is my sister, May.
> A: Is she a student?
> B: Yes, she is. She has a small blue bag.
> A: And who are they?
> B: They are my parents.

**Comprehension questions:**

1. Who is May?  A) A sister  B) A teacher  C) A friend
2. What does May have?  A) A red car  B) A small blue bag  C) Two phones
3. Who are the two other people?  A) Colleagues  B) Parents  C) Teachers

### Dialogue 2: At the office

**Transcript for TTS / recording — keep hidden until learner attempts questions:**

> A: Is this your laptop?
> B: No, it is not my laptop. It is our manager’s laptop.
> A: Where is your laptop?
> B: My laptop is on my desk.

**Comprehension questions:**

1. Who owns the first laptop?  A) The speaker  B) Their manager  C) The receptionist
2. Where is the speaker’s laptop?  A) On the desk  B) At home  C) In a bag

**မြန်မာလို:** Audio ရပြီးမှ မျက်နှာပြင်ကို မကြည့်ဘဲ နားထောင်ပါ။ နှစ်ကြိမ် နားထောင်ပြီးမှ မေးခွန်းတွေ ဖြေပါ။ အသံဖိုင်မရှိသေးရင် ဒီပိုင်းကို Script Reading Practice အနေနဲ့ပဲ သတ်မှတ်ပါ။

## Lesson 4 — Speaking and role-play

**Speak first; correct after finishing.**

1. Show a family photo or imagine one. Say four short sentences: “This is my…”, “He/She is…”, “He/She has…”, and “We…”
2. Role-play: Person A asks “Who is this?” and “Is this your…?” Person B answers, then switch roles.
3. Record 30–45 seconds describing one family member and one colleague. Check have/has and my/his/her.

**Self-review:** Can I speak without stopping for 30 seconds? Are my verbs and pronouns correct? Could another person understand me?
**မြန်မာလို:** အမှားကြောက်ပြီး မရပ်ပါနဲ့။ ပြောပြီးမှ Grammar ကို ပြန်စစ်ပါ။

## Lesson 5 — Reading comprehension

### Passage 1: My family and my work

My name is Min. I live in Yokohama. I have one sister and one brother. My sister is a student. My brother is a designer. I work as a web developer. I have a laptop, and my colleagues have laptops too.

**Questions:**

1. How many siblings does Min have?  A) One  B) Two  C) Three
2. What does Min do?  A) Web developer  B) Teacher  C) Driver
3. Who is a designer?  A) Min’s sister  B) Min’s brother  C) Min’s father

### Passage 2: A new colleague

This is Hana. She is a new colleague. She is kind and friendly. Her desk is next to my desk. She has two notebooks and one phone. We work on the same team.

**Questions:**

1. Is Hana a new colleague?  A) Yes  B) No  C) Not stated
2. How many notebooks does Hana have?  A) One  B) Two  C) Three

**Reading strategy:** Underline the words that directly support your answer before selecting an option.

## Lesson 6 — Writing and editing

1. Write 6–8 sentences about your family or friends. Include two possessive adjectives, two examples of have/has, and at least one plural noun.
2. Edit and rewrite these examples: “My brother have a bike.” “She is my friend. His name is May.” (For a woman named May, use her name/her.)

### Model paragraph (one possible answer)

My name is Aye. I have one brother. His name is Ko. He is a student. My mother is kind. My parents live in Mandalay. I have a laptop. My friends have laptops too.

**မြန်မာလို:** Model answer က တစ်ခုတည်းသော အဖြေမှန် မဟုတ်ဘူး။ ကိုယ့်အကြောင်းနဲ့ ကိုက်ညီအောင် ရေးလို့ရတယ်။ Verb agreement, word order နဲ့ spelling ကို ကိုယ့်ဘာသာ ပြန်စစ်ပါ။

## Lesson 7 — Chapter knowledge check and skill review

### Grammar and vocabulary MCQs (12 questions)

1. My sister ___ a laptop.
   - A) have
   - B) has
   - C) are
2. I ___ two brothers.
   - A) has
   - B) have
   - C) am
3. This is ___ bag. I own it.
   - A) my
   - B) me
   - C) I

   - A) her
   - B) his
   - C) their

   - A) Our
   - B) Us
   - C) We
6. She ___ a teacher.
   - A) has
   - B) are
   - C) is

   - A) have
   - B) has
   - C) is

### Answer key — TEACHER / PRIVATE ONLY

**Never include this section in the public lesson bundle or browser props.**

#### Knowledge check
1. B — has. She / my sister takes has.
2. B — have. I takes have.
3. A — my. Use my before a noun.
4. B — his. His shows possession by Ko in this example.
5. A — Our. Our goes before parents.
6. C — is. Use is for an identity.
7. A — have. They takes have.
8. B — children. Children is the irregular plural.
9. A — a. A singular countable noun needs a determiner.
10. B — her. Her is the possessive adjective before desk.
11. C — are. Plural colleagues takes are.
12. A — He has a bag.. Use has for he and a for one bag.
