## Lesson 2 — Grammar foundations / သဒ္ဒါအခြေခံ

### There is / There are

**English:** There is + one thing: There is a desk. There are + plural things: There are two chairs. Negative: There isn’t a desk. / There aren’t two chairs. Questions: Is there a desk? / Are there any chairs?

**မြန်မာလို:** ရှိတာ တစ်ခုဆို There is၊ အများဆို There are သုံးပါ။ မရှိဘူးဆို isn’t / aren’t၊ မေးခွန်းဆို Is there ...? / Are there ...? သုံးပါ။

### Prepositions of place

**English:** in = inside; on = touching a surface; under = below; next to = beside; between = in the middle of two things; behind = at the back; across from = opposite.

**မြန်မာလို:** in = အတွင်း၊ on = အပေါ်၊ under = အောက်၊ next to = ဘေး၊ between = နှစ်ခုကြား၊ behind = နောက်ဘက်၊ across from = မျက်နှာချင်းဆိုင်။

### A/an and some

**English:** There is a chair. There is an apple on the table. There are some books. Do not use “a” with plural books.

**မြန်မာလို:** တစ်ခုတည်းသော ရေတွက်လို့ရတဲ့ နာမ်ရှေ့ a/an သုံးနိုင်တယ်။ အများကိန်း books ရှေ့မှာ a မသုံးပါနဲ့။

### Common mistakes / အမှားများ

| Incorrect ❌ | Correct ✅ |
|---|---|
| There is two rooms. | There are two rooms. |
| There are a bed. | There is a bed. |
| The laptop is in the desk. | The laptop is on the desk. (if it rests on top) |
| Is there two chairs? | Are there two chairs? |

**Apply:** Correct those sentences without looking. Then say three true sentences about your own life.

## Lesson 3 — Listening preparation / နားထောင်လေ့ကျင့်ရေး

**Audio required:** These are ORIGINAL scripts, not recordings. Once audio is generated, listen twice without the transcript; check answers; then reveal the transcript. Do not record a listening score from reading alone.

### Dialogue 1 — Finding an apartment

**Transcript (hide until after listening):**

- **Mina:** Is there a station near your apartment?
- **Ko:** Yes, there is. It is across from the supermarket.
- **Mina:** Are there any parks nearby?
- **Ko:** Yes, there are two small parks.

**Comprehension checks:**

1. What is near the apartment?
2. How many parks are nearby?

### Dialogue 2 — At home

**Transcript (hide until after listening):**

- **Aye:** Where is my notebook?
- **Su:** It is on the desk, next to your laptop.
- **Aye:** And where is my bag?
- **Su:** It is behind the chair.

**Comprehension checks:**

1. Where is the notebook?
2. Where is the bag?

**Shadowing:** Replay each line and repeat the speaker’s rhythm. / အသံထွက်ပြီးနောက် နောက်ကလိုက်ပြောပါ။

## Lesson 5 — Reading / စာဖတ်ခြင်း

### My new room

My apartment is small, but it is comfortable. There is one bedroom and one kitchen. There are two windows in my bedroom. My desk is next to the window, and my laptop is on the desk. There is a supermarket across from my building. The station is near my home.

**မြန်မာလို လမ်းညွှန်ချက်:** ပထမတစ်ခေါက် အဘိဓာန်မကြည့်ဘဲ ဖတ်ပါ။ ဒုတိယအခေါက် အချက်အလက်တွေကို ရှာပြီး အောက်ပါမေးခွန်းတွေ ဖြေပါ။

**Questions:**

1. Is the apartment large?
2. How many windows are in the bedroom?
3. Where is the desk?
4. What is on the desk?
5. What is across from the building?

## Lesson 6 — Writing / စာရေးလေ့ကျင့်ခြင်း

**Task:** Write 5–7 sentences about your room and neighborhood. Use There is, There are and two prepositions of place.

**မြန်မာလို:** ကိုယ်ပိုင်အခြေအနေကို အသုံးပြုပြီး ညွှန်ကြားချက်အတိုင်း ဝါကျများရေးပါ။ နမူနာကို မကူးပါနဲ့။

**Model for comparison (reveal AFTER writing):**

> There is a desk in my room. My laptop is on the desk. There are two chairs. There is a supermarket near my home. The station is across from a cafe.
