## Lesson 2 — Grammar foundations / သဒ္ဒါအခြေခံ

### Can / Cannot for ability

**English:** I can write HTML. She can speak English. I cannot (can’t) join today. The base verb follows can/can’t: can write, not can writes.

**မြန်မာလို:** လုပ်နိုင်စွမ်းကို can / cannot နဲ့ပြောပါ။ can နောက်မှာ Verb မူရင်းပုံစံသုံးပါ။ He can code. (can codes မဟုတ်)။

### Can for polite requests

**English:** Can you check this page, please? Could you send the file, please? Could you... is often more polite.

**မြန်မာလို:** အကူအညီတောင်းရင် Can you ...? သို့မဟုတ် ပိုယဉ်ကျေးတဲ့ Could you ...? ကို သုံးနိုင်တယ်။

### Simple imperatives

**English:** Open the file. Please check the link. Don’t close this window. Requests sound friendlier with please.

**မြန်မာလို:** ညွှန်ကြားတဲ့အခါ Verb နဲ့စတယ်။ ယဉ်ကျေးဖို့ please ထည့်နိုင်တယ်။ မလုပ်စေချင်ရင် Don’t + verb သုံးပါ။

### Simple time and work updates

**English:** I have a meeting at ten. My task is ready. The deadline is on Friday. I need help with this problem.

**မြန်မာလို:** အလုပ်မှာ ရိုးရှင်းတဲ့ update ပေးနိုင်ဖို့ I have..., My task is..., I need help... စတဲ့ပုံစံတွေသုံးပါ။

### Common mistakes / အမှားများ

| Incorrect ❌ | Correct ✅ |
|---|---|
| He can writes code. | He can write code. |
| Can you to help me? | Can you help me? |
| Please to open the file. | Please open the file. |
| I can not attends. | I cannot attend. |

**Apply:** Correct those sentences without looking. Then say three true sentences about your own life.

## Lesson 3 — Listening preparation / နားထောင်လေ့ကျင့်ရေး

**Audio required:** These are ORIGINAL scripts, not recordings. Once audio is generated, listen twice without the transcript; check answers; then reveal the transcript. Do not record a listening score from reading alone.

### Dialogue 1 — A small task

**Transcript (hide until after listening):**

- **Manager:** Can you update the home page today?
- **Developer:** Yes, I can. Can you send me the new text?
- **Manager:** Of course. I can send it now.
- **Developer:** Thank you. I can finish the page this afternoon.

**Comprehension checks:**

1. What is the task?
2. What does the developer ask for?

### Dialogue 2 — Asking for help

**Transcript (hide until after listening):**

- **Aye:** I have a problem with this file.
- **Min:** Can I help you?
- **Aye:** Yes, please. Could you check the file?
- **Min:** Sure. I can check it after the meeting.

**Comprehension checks:**

1. What is the problem about?
2. When can Min check it?

**Shadowing:** Replay each line and repeat the speaker’s rhythm. / အသံထွက်ပြီးနောက် နောက်ကလိုက်ပြောပါ။

## Lesson 5 — Reading / စာဖတ်ခြင်း

### My workday

I am a junior web developer. I work with a small team. We have a meeting at ten. Today I need to update the home page and check two links. I can update the page, but I cannot fix one problem alone. I ask my teammate for help. Our deadline is Friday.

**မြန်မာလို လမ်းညွှန်ချက်:** ပထမတစ်ခေါက် အဘိဓာန်မကြည့်ဘဲ ဖတ်ပါ။ ဒုတိယအခေါက် အချက်အလက်တွေကို ရှာပြီး အောက်ပါမေးခွန်းတွေ ဖြေပါ။

**Questions:**

1. What is the writer’s job?
2. When is the meeting?
3. What two tasks are mentioned?
4. Can the writer fix every problem alone?
5. When is the deadline?

## Lesson 6 — Writing / စာရေးလေ့ကျင့်ခြင်း

**Task:** Write a simple 5–7 sentence work update and one polite request for help. Include can, cannot, and please.

**မြန်မာလို:** ကိုယ်ပိုင်အခြေအနေကို အသုံးပြုပြီး ညွှန်ကြားချက်အတိုင်း ဝါကျများရေးပါ။ နမူနာကို မကူးပါနဲ့။

**Model for comparison (reveal AFTER writing):**

> I work on a website. Today I can update the home page. I cannot fix one bug alone. The deadline is Friday. Could you check this file, please? Thank you.

**Review:** Underline your subjects and verbs; check punctuation and this chapter’s grammar. Model is only one possible answer. **Not graded or saved by the authoring pack.**
